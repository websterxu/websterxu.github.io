01 / PROFILE

About

Webster Weichu Xu is a tenured Associate Professor in the Department of Business Management at East Stroudsburg University of Pennsylvania, a position held since 2019. He previously served as Assistant Professor at East Stroudsburg University and Framingham State University.

His academic work spans strategic management and international business. His industry experience includes purchasing, supply chain management, and import and export operations in China.
